10.Write a SQL query using a subquery to find all employees whose salary is greater than the average salary 
of all employees.

mysql> with overall_average as(
    -> select avg(salary) as avg_salary
    -> from emp)
    -> select e.emp_name,e.department,e.salary
    -> from emp as e
    -> cross join overall_average oa
    -> on e.salary>oa.avg_salary;
